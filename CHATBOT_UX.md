# Dogcasso — Chatbot-First Interface

## The Vision

The ultimate Dogcasso interface barely needs conventional ecommerce categories.

---

## Conversational Flow

```text
DOG: Who are we making something for?

USER: my girlfriend

DOG: What's coming up?

USER: her 30th

DOG: Tell me about her.

USER: She's called Alice, obsessed with our dachshund Frank,
     always late, loves coffee and Formula 1.

DOG: How much do you want to spend?

USER: £25
```

---

## Curated Response (Not 400 Products)

### £4.99 — Funny

**Alice: The Documentary**
A 15-second mock documentary investigating why Alice has never arrived anywhere on time.

### £12.99 — Personal

**Frank Gives Alice Her Birthday Speech**
Personalized talking-dog movie + custom song + digital card.

### £24.99 — Gift

**The Alice Birthday Pack**
Movie + song + physical QR card + selected coffee/chocolate gift.

---

## Why This Works

| Traditional ecommerce | Dogcasso chatbot |
|----------------------|------------------|
| Search "birthday gift for girlfriend" | "Who is it for?" |
| Scroll 400 products | 3 curated options |
| Compare prices/specs | "How much do you want to spend?" |
| Add to cart, checkout | "Make this one" |
| 45 minutes | 60 seconds |

**That is insanely easier than searching Moonpig/Amazon/Etsy for an hour.**

---

## The Chatbot Knows

Because we captured Alice's profile:
- Name: Alice
- Age: 30
- Relationship: girlfriend
- Pet: Frank (dachshund)
- Interests: coffee, F1
- Humor: sarcastic, always late
- Photos: approved Alice + Frank photos
- Budget indication: £25

**We don't need to ask everything. The profile does the work.**

---

## Progressive Discovery

First interaction:
> "Who is it for?" → "What's coming up?" → "Tell me about them" → curated options

Second interaction:
> "Alice's birthday is in 21 days. Frank has prepared a speech."

Third interaction:
> "For Christmas, we're thinking: Frank as a terrible elf. 'Alice, Frank has reviewed your Christmas performance.'"

**The chatbot gets smarter with each interaction because the profile grows.**

## The Mascot Experience

```text
DOG: Making your movie...

[Dogcasso furiously paints/edits]

[Turns around impatiently]

DOG: Done. £4.99. You're welcome.
```

**Memorable. Differentiating. Brand-building.**

The slightly judgmental painting dog becomes the studio mascot.

> **Dogcasso Studios presents...**

- BREAKING NEWS: JAMIE IS 30
- THE RISE OF SOPHIE
- SANTA WAS HERE
- FRANK'S BIRTHDAY ADDRESS

The brand is not constrained to dogs any more than Moonpig is constrained to pigs.

## Comparison to Moonpig UX

| Moonpig | Dogcasso |
|---------|----------|
| Browse categories | "Who is it for?" |
| Search/filter | "What's coming up?" |
| Compare 100 cards | 3 curated options |
| Customize text | "Tell me about them" |
| Upload photo | Upload photo |
| Add to cart | "Make this one" |
| 20-45 minutes | 60 seconds |

**Moonpig's UX is designed for browsing. Ours is designed for buying.**
