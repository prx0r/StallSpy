# Dogcasso — Customer Strategy

## The Buyer

> **A 24–44-year-old who has somebody's birthday/occasion coming up, has £5–£20 to spend without thinking too hard, and immediately recognizes "Dave would fucking love this."**

Buyer and recipient are often completely different people.

---

## Three Killer Customer Groups

| Buyer | Recipient | What makes them buy | Products |
|-------|-----------|---------------------|----------|
| **Mum/Dad, 28–44** | Child 5–12 | "They will lose their mind when they see this." | Santa, monster room, dinosaur, viral kid meme |
| **Friend/sibling, 18–34** | 18th / 21st / 30th birthday | "This will destroy the group chat." | Breaking News, roast, meme remake, documentary |
| **Partner/family, 28–45** | 30th / 40th / 50th / wedding | "This is funny but actually thoughtful." | Meet Sarah, movie trailer, roast panel, dog speech |

**Pet owners** cut across all three.

---

## #1: Parents of Young Kids

### The insight

Not: "Mum wants a personalized video."

> **Mum can spend £4.99 and make her seven-year-old think Santa was genuinely standing in their living room.**

£5 becomes irrelevant.

### Product test

> "Would this cause a huge reaction?"

**Yes:**
- Santa caught in your house
- Santa says child's name
- Tooth Fairy caught on camera
- Monster in bedroom
- Dinosaur in garden
- Favourite current meme starring the child
- Talking dog tells child happy birthday
- Child becomes action hero/wizard

### Where to reach Mum

| Platform | Reach (UK) | Audience | Use for |
|----------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Facebook | 38.8m (70% adults) | 52.7% female | Parents + retargeting |
| Instagram | 35.5m (63% adults) | 54.2% female | Discovery + slightly older purchaser |
| TikTok | 26.8m (48% adults) | 61.5% female | Discovery/viral demo |

**TikTok is NOT too young for the buyer.** 61.5% female adult audience.

## #2: 18 / 21 / 30 Birthday Groups

### Why this is potentially the best segment

**Buyer and recipient both understand internet humour.**

### Ad concept

> **BREAKING NEWS**
>
> Newsreader: "Authorities have confirmed that Sophie is somehow thirty."
>
> Cut to Sophie stumbling out of nightclub.
>
> Reporter: "Experts had previously believed this impossible."
>
> **Make your mate the headline — £4.99**

### The viral mechanic

Optimize for:

> **Can someone tag/share this with a friend's name without buying it yet?**

Because:

`@jack this is literally you`

is free distribution.

### Milestone variants

Same workflow. Different writing.

| Age | Tone |
|-----|------|
| 18 / 21 | Chaotic, meme-heavy, group-chat, alcohol/night-out humour |
| 30 | **Absolute sweet spot.** "I'm getting old" humour. Friends have disposable income. |
| 40 / 50 | Less trend-dependent. Roasts, family, documentary. Higher AOV. |

## #3: Couples & Family (30th / 40th / 50th / Wedding)

More sentimental.

Higher AOV because the occasion feels more important.

Products: Meet [Name], movie trailer, roast panel, dog speech, sentimental/funny combinations.

## The £5 Psychology

Etsy's Spring/Summer 2026 report: **55% of Gen Z bought a gift in the previous year**, 39% celebrated "just because" moments.

That's exactly where £4.99 sits.

It's not: "Should I buy Dave a £70 gift?"

It's: **"Hahaha. Five quid. Fuck it."**

Design the shop around that impulse.

---

## Product Test (Three Criteria)

Every template must satisfy:

### 1. Recognition
I understand the joke in **2 seconds**.

### 2. Projection
I immediately imagine **a specific person** in it.

### 3. Price irrelevance
£4.99 seems trivial relative to their reaction.

**If all three happen: ship it.**
**If we need a 30-second explanation: kill it.**

---

## Two Creative Feeds

### KIDS / PARENTS

Trend reactive. Track TikTok, Roblox, YouTube Shorts, school memes, Gen-Alpha slang.

When something explodes: "Could a parent insert their child into this?" If yes, make it within 24–48 hours.

**Traditional card companies cannot match this speed.**

### FRIEND GROUP

More evergreen. Core templates: Breaking News, Nature Documentary, The Roast, Courtroom Trial, Movie Trailer, Had To Do It, Corporate Training Film, Sports Press Conference, Meet [Name].

Lightly skin with whatever TikTok joke is current. Organic TikTok is strongest here.

---

## Don't Make £4.99 the True AOV

Cold paid advertising for £4.99 can become difficult economically.

**£4.99 clip = irresistible entry product.**

Checkout upsells:

- +£2.99 Dog Sings Happy Birthday
- +£2 Both Phone + TV formats
- +£2.99 Personalized song
- +£4.99 Physical card + QR
- +£9.99 Upgrade to longer movie

**£4.99 acquisition → £9–£15 average basket.**

Moonpig FY26 AOV: **£9.32**. Gift attachment: **17.9%**.

We approach the same economics from the opposite direction: viral £5 film → attach card/song/gift.

Santa + child trends + magic-in-your-room.
