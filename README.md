## What This Is

A personalized video gift studio. Customers upload photos, we generate studio-grade personalized movies. Not AI slop — curated, refined, character-consistent mini films that become permanent gifts.

## Architecture

5 core engines → unlimited SKUs:

1. **Character Talks To You** — Santa, Witch, Wizard, Vampire speak to camera
2. **Something Was In Your Room** — Monster, Elf, Dinosaur placed in customer's space
3. **Put Me In The Movie** — Customer inserted into cinematic scenarios
4. **Meet [Name]** — 30-60 second narrated mini films
5. **Trend Drop** — Weekly viral format conversions

## Tech Stack

- **MiniMax H3** (primary render engine)
- **LTX-2.5** (fast drafts)
- **ComfyUI** (workflow orchestration)
- **FFmpeg** (post-production)
- **Vast.ai / RunPod** (GPU infrastructure)
- **Prodigi** (physical card fulfillment)
- **Lulu** (book fulfillment, future)
