# MythicBee — Gold Ideas Collection

All key strategic insights from the founding conversation.

---

## Brand & Positioning

> **"We make tiny movies about the people you love."**

> **"Make someone the main character."**

> **"Moonpig has templates where you upload five photos. MythicBee has scenes where you upload five people."**

> **"MythicBee Studios — tiny movies starring your people."**

The name works because Moonpig doesn't make sense either. MythicBee gives us an absurd visual mascot and doesn't trap us into a literal name.

---

## The Category

> **"Don't sell AI videos. Sell personalized versions of internet culture."**

> **"The personalized meme/movie is the acquisition wedge; the real business becomes a gift concierge that remembers your people."**

> **"The product itself is not 'generative video software'; it is a catalog of jokes and moments people instantly want to send to someone."**

---

## The Product Test

> **"Can a buyer see the 3-second demo and immediately think of one specific person to send it to?"**

If yes → launch it.
If no → don't.

**That is probably the single best product test for the whole business.**

---

## UX Philosophy

> **"The winning UX is probably less choice than you're imagining, not more."**

> **"Tell us who it's for. We'll make something they'll actually love."**

> **"Pick joke → Add people → Receive something hilarious."**

> **"Every extra decision creates friction."**

> **"For the default flow, I would not show the full script unless they ask."**

---

## The Retention Engine

> **"The first sale is not the real value; the real asset is the recipient profile."**

> **"Never forget a good gift again."**

> **"Tell us about your people once. Then: we'll make sure you're never boring on their birthday again."**

> **"After a year: 'Tom turns 30 in 21 days. Based on last year's Documentary format, here are 3 new ideas we think he'll love.'"**

> **"That's not something Moonpig or any competitor can replicate quickly."**

---

## The Flywheel

> **"10-30 people see the brand from one purchase."**

> **"A greeting-card purchase normally has one recipient. A funny personalized video gets sent to: family WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, work Slack, group chat."**

> **"Each customer → recipient → new customer."**

---

## The Custom Song

> **"Custom song at 0 cost to us but make it part of a package."**

> **"Marginal cost is tiny, don't necessarily sell it as a standalone £5 product. Use it to make packages feel absurdly valuable."**

> **"Customer sees: video + custom song + card + share page for £14.99 while the extra digital pieces may cost us pennies."**

---

## The Early-Bird Mechanic

> **"It pays to be organised."**

> **"21 days before: 40% early-bird discount. 14 days: 25% off. 7 days: normal price. 48 hours before: Rush production +£4."**

> **"That does two beautiful things: encourages customers to buy before the stressful last minute, and gives us flexibility to batch/render cheaply."**

> **"I'd avoid fake perpetual '50% off today only' discounts. Better to have a real transparent early-bird schedule that customers learn to trust."**

---

## The Recipient Graph

> **"The recipient graph is the moat."**

> **"Moonpig has 113 million occasion reminders. ~40% of orders within 7 days of a reminder. 89.4% of revenue from existing customers."**

> **"We aren't trying to acquire somebody again for each transaction. We acquire Tom → and then Tom adds Alice, Mum, Dad, Dave, Sarah, Frank the dog..."**

> **"That's enormously valuable."**

---

## The Gift Concierge

> **"The chatbot becomes the entire store."**

> **"Who are we making something for?" → "What's coming up?" → "Tell me about her." → "How much do you want to spend?" → Three curated options.**

> **"That is insanely easier than searching Moonpig/Amazon/Etsy for an hour."**

---

## The CAC Solution

> **"Moonpig owns SEO for 'birthday cards.' We advertise on things they don't own: 'funny personalized birthday video,' 'custom birthday movie,' 'santa video for child.'"**

> **"People buy because the demo is funny. Then MythicBee converts those people into card and gift customers later."**

---

## Affiliate Commerce

> **"We don't have to manufacture everything."**

> **"We own the personalization layer while somebody else fulfils the boring commodity."**

> **"The gift recommendation comes from what we actually know about Alice, not merely what other people looking at the same birthday card bought."**

---

## The Defensible Moat

> **"The most defensible version of the whole idea: recurring recipient graph + template library + rendering engine."**

> **"After a year we might have: 500 proven mini-film scenarios rather than 500 greeting-card JPEGs."**

> **"Our catalogue doesn't become obsolete. SANTA_IN_YOUR_ROOM just becomes more convincing."**

---

## Pricing Instinct

> **"£4.99 is an aggressive acquisition price, not necessarily our eventual average order value."**

> **"One customer is already paying $24.99 on Etsy for a personalized 5-7 second animated birthday clip."**

> **"The £29.99 Mini Movie makes £4.99 feel like an impulse purchase."**

---

## The Viral Loop

> **"Recipient opens gift page → watches movie → below it: 'Make one for someone you love — £2 off your first one' → Now recipients become buyers."**

> **"A greeting-card purchase has one recipient. A funny personalized video gets sent to 10-30 people."**

> **"That could make the viral coefficient materially better than physical cards."**

---

## The Brand Mascot

> **"The slightly judgmental painting dog becomes the studio mascot."**

> **"MythicBee is making your movie..." → dog furiously paints/edits → turns around impatiently → "Done. £4.99. You're welcome."**

> **"Not constrained to dogs any more than Moonpig is constrained to pigs."**

---

## Moonpig Validation

> **"Moonpig has already proved almost every boring part of this business: reminders, repeat annual occasions, stored recipient intent, gift attachment, personalization and high returning-customer revenue."**

> **"We don't need to prove people want an online gifting assistant. We need to prove personalized mini-movies/memes are a sufficiently compelling acquisition wedge to get younger customers to start their gifting relationship with MythicBee rather than Moonpig."**

> **"That is a much cleaner thesis."**

---

## The Three Content Lanes

> **"Snapshot of a live scene" is the actual MythicBee format. Memes are just one sub-genre.**

> **Lane 1: Recognisable meme formats** (This Is Fine, Two Buttons)
> **Lane 2: Social-native tropes** (be me, POV, fake trailer, corporate training)
> **Lane 3: Tension/misdirection skits** (CoD door, guilty dog) — no meme-literacy required, purely a filmmaking trick.

> **"Lane 3 might be worth prioritising first since it's the one least dependent on trend timing — it'll still work in a year."**

---

## The Two Independent Axes

> **"You've got two independent axes to test now, not one list."**

> **Axis 1: Scene/Format** (the situation: greentext, POV, nature doc, trailer, group chat)
> **Axis 2: Voice/Register** (the narrator: cinematic epic, ChatGPT-flat, Attenborough-dry, hypebeast-hype)

> **"Cross them and you get way more combinations for basically the same generation cost."**

> **"The strategic question: Is the joke the scene or the voice?"**

---

## The "Be Me" Greentext

> **"Be me. Be 30 tomorrow. Tell everyone you're 'not doing anything big.' Mfw the group chat has 47 unread messages and a Zoom link."**

> **"Cost: basically a TTS voice + one generated image + stock sting. Pennies."**

---

## The ChatGPT Voice Hack

> **"That's not a 'meme format,' that's a voice/register joke, which means you can bolt it onto ANY of the other formats as a caption style."**

> **"'be me' narrated in flat corporate-AI voice instead of movie-trailer voice is a whole different joke from the same scene."**

---

## The Guilty Dog

> **"Zero risk — nothing actually happens, it's a dog watching telly."**

> **"The 'you got me' face is a hugely shareable, recognisable expression on its own."**

> **"Dog IS the punchline — no need to insert recipient's face for demo, which makes it even cheaper to test."**

---

## Tension/Misdirection = Universal

> **"Lane 3 doesn't need meme-literacy at all — pure universal comedic timing, works on anyone regardless of whether they're online enough to get 'distracted boyfriend.'"**

> **"It's a whole separate template category: fear-setup → mundane reveal."**

## The Development Rule

> **"Make the joke → test the joke → only then automate the personalization."**

Prevents building elaborate AI infrastructure before proving anybody cares.

---

## The Ad-Testing Loop

> **"Make the first versions about imaginary characters rather than customers."**

> **"The highest-value signal is probably: tags + shares per 1,000 views — because that directly demonstrates the fundamental MythicBee behaviour."**

> **"50 formats will seem hilarious to us. The market tells us which 5 actually make people reach for their wallet."**

---

## Use the Grammar, Not the Copyright

> **"Don't recreate KC Green's dog exactly. Put Tom at a table drinking tea while everything around him goes catastrophically wrong. Tom is the meme."**

> **"Don't use Simpsons characters. Use the format: lonely kid on school bus staring sadly out the window. Then insert the actual recipient."**

---

## Cost Per Test

> **"Test concepts for pennies in generation cost before doing all the production engineering."**

| Step | Cost |
|------|------|
| Video generation (fictional character) | ~£0.20 |
| Organic posting | £0 |
| Boosting (if traction) | £10-20 |
| **Total per format test** | **£0.20-£10.20** |

**Test 20 formats for under £200. Find the 5 that work.**

---

## The Filter

> **"We will inevitably think 50 formats are hilarious. The market can tell us which five actually make people reach for their wallet."**

> **"If comments immediately fill with 'I NEED THIS FOR MY HUSBAND' then we invest in getting personalization perfect."**
