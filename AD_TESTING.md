# Dogcasso — Ad Testing Loop

## The Development Rule

> **Make the joke → test the joke → only then automate the personalization.**

Prevents building elaborate AI infrastructure before proving anybody cares.

---

---

## Use the Grammar, Not the Copyright

**Ralph Wiggum:** Don't use Simpsons characters. Use the format:

> lonely kid on school bus staring sadly out the window

Then insert the actual recipient.

**Caption:** "When everyone keeps saying 30 isn't old."

Customer recognises the emotional meme format. No copyright issues.

**This Is Fine:** Don't recreate KC Green's dog. Put *Tom* at a table drinking tea while everything around him goes catastrophically wrong.

**Tom is the meme.**

## The Ad-Testing Loop

```text
1. Make generic Dogcasso demo (fictional character, not customer)
    ↓
2. Post organically everywhere (TikTok, IG Reels, FB)
    ↓
3. Measure: tags + shares per 1,000 views
    ↓
4. High traction? → spend £10-20 boosting that specific creative
    ↓
5. See whether people click
    ↓
6. Comments fill with "I NEED THIS FOR MY HUSBAND"?
    ↓
7. Only then build proper customizable Etsy listing
    ↓
8. Feed real customer versions back into advertising (with permission)
```

---

## What We're Looking For

**Not likes.**

**"@Harry this is you"** — tags + shares per 1,000 views.

That directly demonstrates the fundamental Dogcasso behaviour.

---

## Why This Works

- Test concepts for pennies in generation cost before production engineering
- First versions about imaginary characters, not customers
- If format flops: lost £0.20 of video generation
- If comments fill with "I NEED THIS": invest in personalization
- **50 formats will seem hilarious to us. The market tells us which 5 actually make people reach for their wallet.**

---

## Test → Template Pipeline

```text
Organic demo gets traction
    ↓
Spend £10-20 boosting
    ↓
People click? People buy?
    ↓
YES → build proper Etsy listing with personalization
    ↓
Real customer versions → back into advertising (with permission)
    ↓
That template joins the permanent catalogue
```
