# EasyEtsy Product Roadmap & Channel Strategy

## Focus: Video first, cards later

Video is the differentiated wedge. Cards increase AOV and make the gift feel more "real," but they add fulfillment complexity before we've proved the core joke.

---

## Etsy v1: Brutally simple

Start with 4–6 listings:

- **Dog Roasts Owner — £4.99**
- **Breaking News Birthday — £4.99**
- **This Is Fine Birthday — £4.99**
- **Had To Do It Birthday — £4.99**
- **Meet [Name] Mini Movie — £14.99**
- **Monster/Santa Was In Your Room — seasonal**

There are already thousands of personalized video-greeting listings on Etsy, including low-cost AI personalized videos around $5 and much more expensive character/message products, so there is clearly an established buying behavior. ([Etsy][1])

The goal isn't "have lots of products." It is:

> **Get 3 formats with excellent demos, good reviews and repeatable generation.**

Then clone the *engine* into ages/occasions.

Breaking News might become separate search-facing listings for:

**18th Birthday Breaking News**
**21st Birthday Breaking News**
**30th Birthday Breaking News**
**40th Birthday Breaking News**
**50th Birthday Breaking News**

Same backend recipe. Different Etsy keyword intent.

That is where we get catalogue scale cheaply.

---

## Etsy upsells: don't fight the UX

Etsy's new personalization system allows optional **text fields with add-on pricing**, so technically we can offer things like:

> "Add a custom birthday song +£2.99"

through a paid optional field. ([Etsy Help][2])

But digital listings currently **do not support Etsy variations**, which makes elaborate tier selection less elegant than it would be on Dogcasso.com. ([Etsy Help][3])

So keep Etsy upsells minimal:

**Base movie — £4.99**

Optional:

**Personalized song +£2.99**

Maybe one more:

**Priority / longer version +£X**

Don't create a checkout form with twelve upgrades.

## Cards: phase 2

Cards are absolutely worth doing.

But create them as **their own physical Etsy listings**, not one confusing hybrid digital/physical listing.

### Personalized Birthday Card + Custom Movie

**£9.99–£14.99**

Buyer uploads the same photo/details. We generate:

1. matching card artwork;
2. personalized movie;
3. QR on the physical card;
4. production partner prints and sends card.

Etsy explicitly permits print-on-demand/production partners for products based on our original design or the buyer's personalization, provided the production partner is disclosed. ([Etsy Help][4])

QR-related birthday products are already common on Etsy. ([Etsy][5])

That gives us:

> **£4.99 digital film**

versus

> **£12.99 actual card + film**

Much better AOV.

Don't build that fulfillment path **before Dog Roast / Breaking News prove demand**.

## Do NOT add generic dropship gifts to Etsy

Etsy prohibits simply reselling generic commercially available products from wholesalers, outside limited categories. Adding a personalized note/card to an otherwise ordinary wholesale product doesn't make the resale compliant. ([Etsy Help][4])

So:

> film + random wholesale chocolate → no.

But:

- **our custom-designed poster printed by a POD partner** → fine
- **our custom-designed card** → fine
- **our personalized storybook** → fine
- **our personalized mug featuring the generated Dogcasso artwork** → fine (we're creating the design, using a disclosed production partner)

Don't make Dogcasso.com merely a copy of the Etsy shop.

Think of Etsy as:

> **search marketplace + trust + reviews + validation**

> **the actual product.**

## Etsy → website boundary

We can put Dogcasso branding on what we sell, but **do not use Etsy Messages/QR codes to divert an Etsy buyer into paying us off-platform**. Etsy's current seller policy specifically prohibits using external URLs, QR codes, discounts etc. to facilitate an off-platform transaction. ([Etsy][6])

So don't do:

> "Bought on Etsy? Next time get 20% off at dogcasso.com."

inside Etsy communications.

Let the website acquire customers from:

**TikTok / Instagram / Google / organic sharing / our own email opt-ins**

while Etsy remains its own marketplace channel.

## Product roadmap

### Phase 1 — prove the joke

**Digital videos only.** 4–6 excellent listings.

Goal: **first 20–50 paid orders + reviews.**

### Phase 2 — increase basket

Add: **songs, poster/still, digital card.**

All basically zero-inventory.

### Phase 3 — physical

Add: **QR greeting card, poster, storybook** through production partners.

### Phase 4 — Dogcasso.com becomes primary product

Accounts, recipient profiles, reminders, voice intake, gift pages.

### Phase 5 — broader gifting

Affiliate/partner gifts, flowers, chocolates, experiences, etc. **on Dogcasso.com**, where Etsy's resale rules aren't dictating the business model.

That separation is clean:

> **Etsy = personalized creative products.**
> **Dogcasso.com = personalized gifting assistant.**

Absolutely resist doing cards *before* we have one genuinely killer Dog Roast or Breaking News demo. The video is what gets Dogcasso noticed; the card is how we monetize that attention better.
