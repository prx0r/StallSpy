## Early-Bird Pricing Schedule

> **It pays to be organised.**

| Timing | Discount | Effect |
|--------|----------|--------|
| 21+ days before | 40% off | Encourage early purchase, batch rendering |
| 14-20 days before | 25% off | Still good value, less urgency |
| 7-13 days before | Normal price | Standard |
| 48 hours before | +£4 rush fee | Premium for last-minute |

**Why this works:**
- Real transparent schedule customers learn to trust (not fake perpetual discounts)
- Encourages purchase before stressful last minute
- Gives us flexibility to batch/render cheaply
- Rush fee captures value from panic buyers

## Referral Loop

Recipient opens gift page:
`dogcasso.com/g/tom-30th`

Watches movie. Below it:

> **Make one for someone you love — £2 off your first one**

**Now recipients become buyers.**

### Why this beats greeting cards

A greeting-card purchase has **one recipient**.

A funny personalized video gets sent to:
- family WhatsApp
- Instagram
- TikTok
- work Slack
- group chat

**10-30 people see the brand from one purchase.**

Viral coefficient materially better than physical cards.

## Defensibility

The most defensible version of Dogcasso is:

**Recipient graph** (who they buy for)
+ **Template library** (500+ proven scenarios)
+ **Rendering engine** (improves yearly)
+ **Personalization history** (what worked before)

After a year:

> "Tom turns 30 in 21 days. Based on last year's Documentary format, here are 3 new ideas we think he'll love."

**That's not something Moonpig or any competitor can replicate quickly.**
