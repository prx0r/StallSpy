# Dogcasso — Partnerships & Affiliate Commerce

## Core Principle

We don't have to manufacture everything.

Dogcasso's high-margin proprietary products sit at the centre. Partnerships extend the offering without operational complexity.

## The Chatbot Recommends Bundles

When Alice loves coffee:

> **"Movie + physical card + coffee gift"**

We own the **personalization layer** while somebody else fulfils the boring commodity.

The chatbot's recommendation comes from what we actually know about Alice — not merely what other people looking at the same birthday card bought.

---

## Moonpig Validation

Moonpig is aggressively pursuing exactly this attach strategy:
- Gifts attached to **17.9% of orders in FY26**
- Adding partners: Next, Boots, flowers, homeware, fragrance
- Stated strategy: increase AOV by attaching relevant gifts to card journey

**Our twist is stronger: recommendations based on actual recipient knowledge.**

## Bundle Architecture

```text
DOG: Alice loves coffee, right?
     Want us to add a coffee gift?

     [+£12.99] A bag of Alice's favourite
               from [Partner Roaster]

USER: yes

DOG: Done. Total: £37.98
     Alice gets: movie + card + coffee.
     You get: zero stress.
```

**The personalization layer is ours. The fulfillment is shared.**

## Revenue Model

| Revenue stream | % of total (mature) | Margin |
|---------------|---------------------|--------|
| Digital products (clips, movies, songs) | 50% | 90%+ |
| Physical cards | 25% | 80% |
| Gift bundles (card + third-party) | 15% | 40-60% (blended) |
| Pure affiliate (no Dogcasso product) | 10% | 15-25% |

**The high-margin digital products subsidize the physical/affiliate offering.**

---

## Why Partners Come to Us

1. **Recipient knowledge** — We know Alice likes coffee, not just "people who buy birthday cards"
2. **High-intent traffic** — Someone buying a personalized movie is already in gift-buying mode
3. **Younger demographic** — Under-55s, parents, group-chat sharers
4. **Viral exposure** — 10-30 people see each gift shared in group chats
5. **Repeat engagement** — 89%+ returning customers (Moonpig benchmark)

---

## The Strategy

**Own the personalization layer. Partner for everything else.**

Dogcasso doesn't need warehouses, inventory, or logistics.

We need:
- GPU infrastructure (we control)
- Personalization engine (we control)
- Recipient graph (we control)
- Recommendation engine (we control)
- Partners for physical fulfillment (they control)

**That's a software business with physical reach.**
