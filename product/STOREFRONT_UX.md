# MythicBee — Storefront UX

## Core Principle

Don't make the user think: "What can artificial intelligence make?"

Make them think: "Holy shit, Dave would find that hilarious."

---

## The Purchase Impulse

The purchasing impulse is basically exactly what greeting cards monetize:

> Browse thumbnails → "Dave would love this" → click → upload Dave → £4.99 → send to group chat

**Speed matters.** The entire flow should take under 2 minutes from discovery to purchase.

---

## Demo Videos

Every listing has a **10-second demo** showing:
1. The concept (using stock/example photo)
2. The quality
3. The joke

Customer immediately understands what they're buying.

No abstract "AI video" language. Just: "This is what your friend gets."

---

## Upsell Presentation

After selecting a base product:

```text
YOUR MOVIE — £4.99

Make it better:
  [+£2.00] Add HD + both formats
  [+£4.99] Send as a real card
  [+£5.00] Add a custom song
  [+£15.00] Upgrade to 30-sec mini movie
  [+£20.00] Turn into a printed storybook
```

**Show the value, not the tech.**

## Mobile-First Design

- 9:16 preview videos (native to mobile scroll)
- Tap to upload (camera roll integration)
- One-tap WhatsApp share
- Apple Pay / Google Pay checkout
- No account required for first purchase
