# MythicBee — Purchase Flow

## Core Principle

> **Tell us who it's for. We'll make something they'll actually love.**

Less choice, not more. Every extra decision creates friction.

---

## The 9-Step Flow

1. **Who's it for?**
   - Name, relationship (friend/mum/partner/etc)

2. **What's the occasion?**
   - Birthday / Christmas / Halloween / Just Because / Other

3. **Upload 1-5 photos**
   - Face photos, room photos, pet photos
   - Voice input works for descriptions

4. **Tell us 2-3 things about them**
   - Voice input works perfectly here
   - "He's obsessed with his dog, always late, loves Formula 1"

5. **Pick tone**
   - Funny / Sweet / Savage / Unhinged

6. **MythicBee shows ONE strong concept preview**
   - Not ten options
   - One concept, thumbnail, 3-second demo

7. **Buttons:**
   - Love it / Make it funnier / Try another

8. **Pay**
   - One click, minimal friction

9. **Receive MP4 immediately or same day**

---

## Advanced Controls (Hidden by Default)

Behind **"Want to tweak it?"**:

- Edit script
- Change joke
- Swap character
- Make shorter
- Make meaner
- Make sentimental
- Landscape / vertical / both

**Most buyers should never need them.**

## Physical Card Delivery Options

- **Digital now** — immediate MP4
- **Physical card arrives tomorrow / selected date**

MythicBee becomes useful even for people who forgot the birthday until today.

---

## The Product Test

For every new template:

> **Can a buyer see the 3-second demo and immediately think of one specific person to send it to?**

If yes, it belongs in MythicBee.

If no — if they think "that's cool" but can't name a specific recipient — it doesn't pass.

**That is probably the single best product test for the whole business.**
