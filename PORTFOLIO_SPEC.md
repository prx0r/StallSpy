# Dogcasso Portfolio — The 20-Store Strategy

**Date:** 5 September 2026
**Status:** Strategic thesis — scoping the studio portfolio
**Core insight:** One factory, many studios. The long-term asset isn't twenty shops — it's a personalization factory.

---

## The Formula

> **One shop = one fantasy the buyer can understand in five seconds.**

Not one shop per product category. Not "AI gifts." A shopper should land and immediately think: *these are the people who do this exact thing.*

The deeper formula tying all twenty together:

**known desire**
× **recognizable cultural artifact**
× **specific person**
× **specific occasion**
× **AI capability that wasn't practical two years ago**

## The Promotion System

### How a concept graduates to its own shop

**Stage 0 — Idea**
> Dad scores the World Cup winner.

It stays merely an idea.

**Stage 1 — Template earns validation**
Produce the recipe across 5-10 difficult inputs:
- young / old / bald / glasses / bad phone image / full body / portrait crop / different skin tones

Target: **≥80-90% acceptable outputs** with **≤1-1.5 paid generations/order**.

**Stage 2 — Commercial mockup**
Full customer experience:
> original photo → hero portrait → video → commentary → physical mockup → card

Answer: *Would somebody understand why this is a great gift within five seconds?*

If not, kill or revise.

**Stage 3 — Concept earns a specialist store**
Create the specialist Etsy shop **before** accumulating meaningful sales in the parent shop.

Launch 5 listings maximum. All variants of one engine:

```
SPORTS_HERO_V1 → football / cricket / baseball / basketball / retirement
```

**Stage 4 — Data-driven promotion (30-60 days / ~500 qualified visits)**

Track:

| Metric | What It Tells You |
|--------|-------------------|
| Impressions / search terms / CTR | Demand |
| Favourites / carts / purchases | Purchase intent |
| Generation cost / retry rate | Production economics |
| Review rate / shares / repeat | Customer delight |
| Film → card / mug attach rate | Upsell potential |

### Four outcomes

| Outcome | Criteria | Action |
|---------|----------|--------|
| **Promote** | Strong demand + strong reliability | More variants, own social, better website, physical products, ad budget |
| **Maintain** | Profitable but niche | Leave listings running, backend is automatic |
| **Merge** | Good product, insufficient brand reason | Move into related studio |
| **Kill** | No demand / expensive / confusing | No sunk-cost loyalty |

## Why Game Winner Skips a Stage

Most ideas need validation before getting a shop.

Game Winner passes four unusually important tests:

1. **One-sentence comprehension:** "We put him into the sporting moment he's always dreamed about."
2. **Huge recipient problem:** "Gifts for Dad / husband / boyfriend are difficult."
3. **Existing purchasing category:** Sports gifts are already normal. No new behavior required.
4. **AI actually changes the product:** Without current AI video, Dad *cannot* score the winning goal with personalized commentary.

That's exactly where AI products should live.
