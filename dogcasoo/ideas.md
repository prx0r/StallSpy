# Dogcasso Gift Engines — Core Idea

## The crucial move: separate the fantasy engine from the occasion

You do not build a Father's Day product and then another birthday product. You build **"Dad Wins the World Final" once**, then sell the exact engine as:

- Dad birthday gift
- Father's Day gift
- 40th/50th/60th birthday
- retirement gift
- Christmas gift for Dad
- gift from son/daughter
- even groom/best-man versions

Etsy says shopping by **recipient** is a major pattern, personalization is especially important for Mother's/Father's Day, and its 2026 report explicitly groups birthdays, anniversaries, retirements and other milestones alongside the major holidays. ([Etsy][1])

## Father's Day / Dad birthday: DAD: THE GAME WINNER

Customer supplies:

- Dad photo
- country/team colours
- sport
- name
- optional year/age
- one or two family jokes

We generate:

### 1. Hero portrait

A ridiculous premium sports-card image.

**DAVID — LEGENDARY SEASON**

Position: Striker
Appearances: 1
Goals: 1
Most important goals: 1

### 2. The film

15–20 seconds. Huge stadium. 89:58 on clock. Ball falls to Dad.

Commentator: "It has to be Prior… PRIORRRRRRR!"

Goal. Stadium erupts. Dad kneeslides in front of thousands of people. Commentator completely loses it.

### 3. Custom audio

Crowd chanting his name. Possibly even a ridiculous little national/stadium song.

### 4. Physical gift

Large premium mug / tumbler + printed hero card.

Prodigi already has photo mugs and large ceramic mugs fulfilled in both the UK and US, while its US catalogue includes tumblers, koozies, golf balls, playing cards and other Dad-adjacent products. ([Prodigi][2])

Pricing:

- **£7.99 digital**
- **£19.99 gift bundle**
- **£29.99 premium bundle**

Listing preview of Dad scoring the winner is the advert. Much stronger than another #1 Dad mug.

## Mother's Day / Mum birthday: MUM: THE ALBUM

Upload Mum. Tell us:

- her name
- children/partner/pets
- favourite music era
- three memories/in-jokes
- age/occasion

Dogcasso makes:

### Album cover

**JULIE** — *The Kids Were Eventually* — Deluxe 60th Anniversary Edition

### Actual song

A surprisingly decent personalized song. Could be 70s disco, 80s synth pop, 90s ballad, indie, country, soul — whatever suits her.

### Music video

15-sec hero excerpt using her portrait.

### Physical product

Framed album cover / mug / card.

Birthday: **Julie — Greatest Hits: 1966–2026**
Mother's Day: **Mum — Greatest Hits**
Christmas: **The Christmas Sessions**

Same engine. Because the song itself is playable, this has **massively higher perceived effort** than a portrait.

## Mother / daughter: TAROT

Personalized major-arcana-style card. Symbols derived from her: children, birth flowers, hometown, hobbies, pets, personality.

Or ridiculous:

**THE WORRIER**

Upright: care, preparedness, packed sandwiches
Reversed: "Text me when you get there."

That's a Mother's Day card people would actually show other people. Then Dogcasso brings it alive.

## Birthday: the biggest universal category

Holidays come once annually. **Birthdays happen every day.** Every successful engine should have an evergreen birthday version.

- **Dad Birthday** → Dad Scores The Winner
- **Mum Birthday** → Mum's Greatest Hits
- **Partner Birthday** → Your Relationship: The Album
- **Best Friend Birthday** → WANTED: [NAME] — Crimes accumulated over 20 years
- **Kid Birthday** → Legend Card — Make them an original game hero
- **Pet-owner Birthday** → A Birthday Message From Their Pet
- **Funny adult birthday** → [NAME]-O'S Cereal
- **Milestone birthday** → The Definitive Documentary — "After fifty years in the wild, scientists are beginning to understand Dave."

## Natural Habitat: the universal Dogcasso template

- **Dad** → *Homo paternalus* — Commonly observed standing over a barbecue giving unsolicited instructions.
- **Mum** → *Mater omniscientia* — Remarkably capable of locating objects other family members insist are missing.
- **Boyfriend** → Commonly seen migrating between sofa, refrigerator and PlayStation.
- **Cat** → A solitary apex predator whose daily hunt largely consists of waiting for somebody else to open a tin.
- **Toddler** → Highly energetic. Territorial. Difficult to reason with.
- **Grandad** → Can remain motionless in one armchair for extraordinary periods.

Every one becomes: **portrait + specimen card + documentary video + narrated audio.** That's a real franchise format.

## "Hero" package: sports across cultures

- **Football** → score winning World-final-style goal
- **Baseball** → bottom of the ninth → walk-off homer
- **Basketball** → buzzer-beater championship shot
- **Golf** → impossible final putt
- **Tennis** → championship point
- **Boxing** → championship ring walk + victory
- **Motorsport** → crosses finish line first

Avoid official league/team marks. Reproduce the **fantasy**, not licensed branding.

Localization:

- US shop: baseball / basketball / American football
- UK: football / rugby / cricket
- Australia: cricket / Aussie rules
- India: Dad hits the winning six

Globally reusable engine.

## Three price bands

### Impulse — £4.99–£8.99

Digital artifact + animation. Perfect Etsy acquisition.

### Proper Gift — £15–£30

Digital experience + physical artifact. Probably where you want most customers.

### "Holy shit, look at this" — £30–£60

Physical piece + longer film + custom music + perhaps AR.

Once your template works, the production difference between £8 and £40 can be surprisingly small.

---

## Don't create 40 production systems

Make **six**:

1. **PORTRAIT**
2. **SPORTS HERO**
3. **ALBUM**
4. **TAROT/LEGEND CARD**
5. **NATURAL HABITAT**
6. **WANTED**

Everything else initially is a reskin.

The Etsy shop can have dozens of tightly targeted listings:

- Dad birthday football gift
- Father's Day football gift
- 60th birthday football gift
- personalized cricket gift for Dad
- baseball gift for husband
- retirement gift for football fan

while under the hood they're all: `SPORTS_HERO_V1`

Boring operational leverage that makes Dogcasso viable rather than another sprawling AI project.

If we validate just Sports Hero + Album + Natural Habitat, we already have Dad birthday, Father's Day, Mum birthday, Mother's Day, partner birthday, retirement and Christmas covered without building seven separate businesses.
