# Dogcasso — Advertising Strategy

## Channel Priority

| Channel | Use | Verdict |
|---------|-----|---------|
| **TikTok organic** | viral demos/memes | **#1** |
| **Meta Reels: FB + IG together** | cheap paid testing/conversions | **#1 paid** |
| TikTok paid | scale proven viral creative | #2 |
| Instagram-only ads | unnecessary initially | #3 |
| Facebook feed-only | parents/Santa retargeting | useful later |

## First Paid Spend

> **UK, broad 23–44, Meta sales campaign, Advantage+ placements, £10–£20/day, one genuinely funny 9:16 video.**

Don't overly target interests yet.

Publish same video organically to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

## The Dog Ad

Fancy interview set. Dog behind microphone.

Interviewer: "So what do you really think of your owner?"

Dog: "Yeah, I also think he's weird…"

Beat.

"…but someone has to love him, am I right?"

Studio audience laughs.

**DOGCASSO.COM**

Tiny line: **Make your dog roast you — £4.99**

**Why it works:** Someone scrolls thinking "funny talking dog clip", not "here comes an ecommerce advertisement."

**Better:** Use an actual Dogcasso order as the ad. Then the product advertises itself.

---

## The Advertising Loop

A Dogcasso product should simultaneously be:

**the product** and **the ad for the product.**

```text
Person sees dog making owner joke
    ↓
Laughs
    ↓
Only at the end: Make yours — dogcasso.com — £4.99
    ↓
Person tags friend
    ↓
Friend sends it
    ↓
Recipient posts finished version
    ↓
Their followers see Dogcasso
    ↓
More people discover Dogcasso
```

**Prioritize organic TikTok + Instagram Reels before spending money.**

When one organic clip outperforms → that's the one to put paid budget behind.

Substantially smarter for a £4.99 product than blindly spending £30/day testing mediocre ads.

---

## Creative Rules

1. **First 5 seconds should not look like an ad**
2. **9:16 native video with sound** (materially lower cost-per-result than images)
3. **Make someone tag a friend without buying yet** → free distribution
4. **The demo IS the ad** — if the concept isn't funny in 3 seconds, it won't work as an ad either
5. **Use Advantage+ placements** — Meta distributes across FB/IG/Reels/Messenger

---

## Trend Radar → Ad Pipeline

```text
TikTok Creative Center (free)
    ↓
Identify trending format
    ↓
Make Dogcasso version within 24-48 hrs
    ↓
Post organically (TikTok + IG Reels + FB)
    ↓
Measure performance
    ↓
If outperforms → put paid budget behind it
    ↓
Scale until engagement drops
    ↓
Next trend
```

**TikTok's Creative Center explicitly exposes current trends, hashtags and high-performing ads.**

## Retargeting (Later)

|----------|-----|
| Website visitors who didn't buy | Show them the dog ad again |

---

## Budget Progression

| Phase | Monthly spend | Focus |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| **Launch** | £0 | Organic TikTok + IG Reels |
| **Test** | £300-600 | Meta Reels, one proven creative |
| **Scale** | £1,000-3,000 | Multiple proven creatives, TikTok paid |
| **Mature** | £3,000-10,000 | Full funnel, retargeting, seasonal spikes |

**Never spend paid money on unproven creative.**
