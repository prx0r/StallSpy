# Dogcasso — Trend Radar System

Identify emerging internet culture → convert into personalized video templates → exploit for 2-6 weeks → retire.

**Pipeline:** identify trend Monday → template Tuesday → Etsy/TikTok listing Wednesday → exploit → retire.

## Evaluation Criteria

For each potential trend, ask:

### 1. Can we personalize it?
- Does it have a repeatable structure?
- Can we swap in customer photos/names/rooms?
- Does it work with our 5 engines?

### 2. Is it legally safe?
- Can we recreate the grammar without using copyrighted material?
- Are we making the customer the meme, not selling someone else's IP?
- Can we use our own music/characters/scripts?

### 3. Does it have shelf life?
- Emerging (last 1-2 weeks) → hot, launch fast
- Growing (2-6 weeks) → good window
- Mature (6+ weeks) → one product, don't build around it
- Dead → skip

### 4. Can we execute it with current tech?
- Face insertion needed? → Engine 3
- Room/environment needed? → Engine 2
- Character performance needed? → Engine 1
- Just script + format? → Engine 5

## Trend Categories

### Evergreen (Classics Layer)
Meme grammars that persist for years. Always available.
- Had To Do It To Em
- Disaster Girl
- Distracted Boyfriend
- etc.

### Seasonal (Occasions Layer)
Predictable annual peaks.
- Christmas: Oct-Dec
- Halloween: Sep-Oct
- Birthdays: year-round
- Weddings: year-round
- Graduation: May-Jul
- Mother's/Father's Day: Mar/May

### Viral (Trending Layer)
Unpredictable, fast-moving. Must identify and execute within days.
- TikTok format of the week
- Reddit viral moment
- Celebrity/news moment (if remixable)
