### The Review-to-Price Relationship
- Under $20: converts with 5-10 reviews
- $20-50: needs 15-30 reviews
- Above $50: needs 50+ reviews
- Dropping from 5.0 to 4.7 stars has measurable impact
- Below 4.5 stars = catastrophic

## 5. The Dogcasso Gap — What Nobody Else Does

### Current Market Structure

```
TALKING PET VIDEOS          CUSTOM PORTRARDS          GREETING CARDS
($5-20, digital only)       ($10-150, digital/print)  (£2-10, physical only)
        │                           │                         │
        │                           │                         │
        └───────────┬───────────────┘                         │
                    │                                         │
              NOBODY COMBINES ALL THREE                       │
                    │                                         │
                    └─────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                    │
                              DOGCASSO
                         (video + meme + card)
```

### What Competitors Don't Do

1. **No meme-format video gifts** — existing talking pet videos are generic "dog says happy birthday" not structured comedy
2. **No physical + digital bundles** — nobody offers card + video as one product
3. **No age-milestone × format matrix** — nobody has 30th/40th/50th × breaking news/two buttons/etc.
4. **No "become the meme" positioning** — existing products are "your pet talks" not "you become the meme"
5. **No recipe library** — no competitor has a systematic template → listing pipeline

### The Competitive Moat

Dogcasso's moat is NOT the technology (anyone can use H3). It's:

1. **Template system** — one engine × many occasions = many listings
2. **Meme grammar expertise** — knowing which formats work and why
3. **Card + video bridge** — physical product increases perceived value 3x
4. **Etsy SEO coverage** — 20+ listings from 4 templates dominate search
5. **Review velocity** — early reviews compound into conversion advantage
