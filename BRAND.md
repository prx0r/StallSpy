# Dogcasso — Brand Guide

## Name

**Dogcasso**

Less literal. Easy to remember. Sounds creative. Not trapped in "AI Video Generator 24."

Moonpig doesn't make sense either. Dogcasso gives us an absurd visual mascot and doesn't trap us into a literal name.

---

## Positioning

> **Dogcasso Studios — tiny movies starring your people.**

The name makes sense retroactively: Dogcasso is the weird little studio making them.

---

## Taglines

Primary:
> **Make someone the main character.**

Secondary:
> **You provide the people. We make the movie.**

Studio variant:
> **Dogcasso Studios presents...**

---

## The Category

> **Moonpig has templates where you upload five photos. Dogcasso has scenes where you upload five people.**

That is the distinction.

---

## The Mascot

The slightly judgmental painting dog.

- Appears in marketing
- Breaks the fourth wall
- Part of checkout/loading experience
- "Dogcasso is making your movie..." → dog furiously paints/edits → turns around impatiently → "Done. £4.99. You're welcome."

**Memorable. Differentiating. Brand-building.**

Not constrained to dogs any more than Moonpig is constrained to pigs.

---

## Consumer Experience

**Aggressively simple.**

- No model names
- No timeline editor
- No prompt engineering
- No AI jargon

**Pick joke → Add people → Receive something hilarious.**

---

## Voice

- Warm, funny, slightly irreverent
- Never "AI-generated" in customer-facing copy
- The dog mascot has personality
- "Funny. Magical. Personal. £4.99."

---

## How We Talk About Ourselves

**We are:** A tiny movie studio.
**We make:** Personalized films starring the people you love.
**We're not:** An AI video generator. A prompt box. A editing tool.

**What the customer does:** Uploads photos, answers a few questions.
**What we do:** Everything else.

---

## Template-Driven (Not Prompt-Driven)

Each template specifies its own input contract:

```text
ROAST_FAMILY_V1

Required:
- recipient face
- Mum face
- Dad face

Optional:
- dog photo

Questions:
- age
- 3 embarrassing facts
- running joke
- roast intensity

Outputs:
- 9:16
- 16:9
```

**Much better than "upload anything and prompt anything."**

Later, offer tiny controlled modifications:
- Make it meaner
- Make it wholesome
- Swap Mum for the dog
- Change ending
- Use this joke instead

**That preserves reliability.**
