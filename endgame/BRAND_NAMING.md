# Brand Naming — Parent Company Analysis

**Date:** 5 September 2026

---

## The Problem

The portfolio contains two product families:

**A. Elevation products** — Make them iconic
Dogcasso, GameWinners, movie posters, album covers, superheroes, magazine covers, fantasy portraits

**B. Memory products** — Make it last
Family annual, couple yearbook, grandparent biography, recipe book, private crossword, memory game, Christmas story, star maps

The parent needs to sit above both fame and memory.

---

## Three Strongest Conceptual Territories

### 1. MUSE

The person/story is the inspiration. Best when you want the brand to feel creative, premium and AI-native without saying AI.

> Inspired by yours. Made one of one.

### 2. MOMENT

Something meaningful happened; we turn it into something permanent. Best for gifting/emotional breadth.

> Make the moment last.

### 3. ICON

We elevate ordinary people/moments into something larger-than-life. Best for more viral/fun visual products.

> Make them iconic.

---

## Why Muse Wins

Muse can produce:
- an iconic transformation
- OR a sentimental keepsake

Without semantic strain. It sits above both families naturally.
