# The Daily Operating Ritual

**Date:** 5 September 2026
**Constraint:** Spend almost zero time "making content." Actual job is build and sell. System observes and manufactures documentation.

---

## The Complete Day

```text
MORNING
belief + goal + planned experiment
        ↓
DAY
raw work / OpenCode / Git / commerce
        ↓
EVENING
metrics + money + 60-sec reflection
        ↓
NIGHTLY AGENT
structured corpus extraction
        ↓
PUBLIC
blog + Short + scoreboard
        ↓
LATER
1d / 7d / 30d outcomes attached
```

---

## 1. Do the Work Normally

- OpenCode sessions
- Etsy changes
- StallSpy research
- Image/video generation
- Supplier work
- Git commits
- Customer interactions
- Spend/revenue

---

## 2. Capture Automatically During the Day

- Full OpenCode traces
- Git diffs
- Model/tool calls
- AI/GPU spend
- Listing revisions
- Etsy/store metrics
- Financial transactions
- Generated assets
- Screenshots where useful

---

## 3. At End of Day, Export All OpenCode Sessions

Keep raw exports unchanged as the permanent evidence layer.

---

## 4. Take One Business-State Snapshot

Critical:

- Cash
- Daily revenue
- Cumulative revenue
- Profit
- Visits
- Conversion
- Orders
- Active listings
- Current experiments
- Current hypotheses
- Generation reliability

---

## 5. Record a 30–90 Second Human Voice/Video Note

> "Today I thought X would work. It didn't. Game Winner looks much stronger because Y. Tomorrow I'm testing Z."

Gives the corpus information that may never appear in Git or agent transcripts: uncertainty, intuition, frustration, surprise, reasoning.

---

## 6. Run the Nightly Extraction Agent

Gets:
- OpenCode traces
- Git history
- Metrics
- Financials
- Voice-note transcript
- Screenshots/assets
- Previous day's state

Produces:
- `workday.json`
- Semantic session summaries
- Decisions
- Hypotheses
- Actions
- Unresolved questions
- Candidate lessons
- Mistakes/recoveries
- Next-day plan
- Public-safe version
- Private corpus version

---

## 7. Generate Daily Public Outputs

- 1 blog post
- 1 YouTube Short
- Optionally same Short to TikTok/Reels
- Updated public scoreboard

---

## 8. Don't Declare Lessons as Truth Immediately

Nightly agent says:

> "Candidate lesson: buyers care more about occasion than novelty."

Stays `candidate` until later evidence supports it.

---

## 9. Attach Outcomes Later

Day 17: Changed hero image.
Day 24: CTR +31%.
Day 47: Effect persisted.

Jobs automatically enrich old actions with:
- 1-day outcome
- 7-day outcome
- 30-day outcome
- Final assessment

---

## 10. Weekly Synthesis (Every 7 Days)

- One longer YouTube video
- One weekly research report
- Experiment leaderboard
- P&L
- Hypotheses supported/rejected
- What changed in worldview
- Next week's bets

---

## Three Levels of Content

```text
RAW
Every action / trace / metric

DAILY
What happened and what we currently think

WEEKLY
What appears to actually matter

MONTHLY / QUARTERLY
General principles and playbooks
```

---

## Morning Record (From Day 1)

Before starting work:

```text
TODAY'S GOAL
What is the single most important outcome?

CURRENT BELIEF
What do I think will work?

MAIN BET
What am I testing?

SUCCESS CONDITION
What result would make me continue?

KILL CONDITION
What result would make me stop?
```

Nightly agent otherwise has to infer intentions retrospectively.

---

## What You Get After One Year

Not just ~365 Shorts and ~365 blog posts.

You get:
- 365 reconstructed business states
- Hundreds of agent trajectories
- Thousands of decisions/actions
- Outcome-linked evidence about what actually happened next

**That's the asset.**
