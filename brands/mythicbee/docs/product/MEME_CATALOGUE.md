## 1. Had To Do It Birthday — Difficulty: ★

One uploaded person. Walks into shot. Stops. Pose. Freeze.

> **"You know James had to turn 30 on 'em."**

Very reliable. No multi-character interaction.

---

## 2. Breaking News Birthday — Difficulty: ★

Face + name + age + funny fact.

Studio anchor → field reporter → ridiculous reconstruction → birthday sting.

One workflow. Hundreds of products.

---

## 3. Dog Roasts Owner — Difficulty: ★

Dog photo. Dog "speaks" about owner.

> "Yeah, I also think he's weird… but someone has to love him, am I right?"

Demonstrates MythicBee's personality immediately.

---

## 4. Nature Documentary — Difficulty: ★

3–5 photos + facts.

Distinguished narrator: "Here we observe Tom in his natural habitat."

> "Despite three decades of evolution, he remains incapable of arriving on time."

---

## 5. Caught Looking — Difficulty: ★★

Adaptation of Distracted Boyfriend.

**How to solve the 3-person challenge:**

Construct an ideal first frame ourselves:

```text
recipient ---- partner
       walking →

              distraction
                 ← walking
```

Image-to-video with only:

> third subject walks past; recipient turns head following them; partner looks at recipient in disbelief.

Freeze frame and labels are deterministic editing, not generated.

Call it **"Caught Looking"** — not the stock-photo name.

---

## 6. This Is Fine Birthday — Difficulty: ★★

One recipient sitting peacefully drinking tea.

Everything behind them progressively goes wrong.

> **Tom preparing for his wedding tomorrow.**

Visual chaos is something video models are good at.

---

## 7. Two Buttons Birthday — Difficulty: ★★

Recipient sweating dramatically. Two giant buttons.

> **Act my age** vs. **Order another pint**

Most buttons/text composited afterward.

---

## 8. Birthday Confessional — Difficulty: ★

Person looks deadpan off-camera:

> "Do I think Sarah is getting old?"

Cut.

> "Yes."

Cut to Sarah violently opening champagne.

**HAPPY 30TH**

Etormally funny. No copyrighted TV show dependency.

---

## 9. Success Kid — Difficulty: ★

Upload kid/adult. Person walks into dramatic scene. Tiny fist pump. Background goes absurdly heroic.

> **SURVIVED 30 YEARS**

No multi-character interaction required.

---

## 10. Brainrot / Current Trend — Difficulty: varies

Changes every week.

Track TikTok, Roblox, YouTube Shorts, school memes, Gen-Alpha slang.

When something explodes: "Could a parent insert their child into this?" If yes, make within 24–48 hours.

**Traditional card companies cannot match this speed.**

---

## Difficulty Guide

| Stars | What it means |
|-------|--------------|
| ★ | One subject, simple action, reliable generation |
| ★★ | Multiple subjects OR specific choreography OR visual complexity |
| ★★★ | Three+ subjects, precise timing, high reroll risk |

**Start with ★ products. They're the most reliable and commercially proven.**
