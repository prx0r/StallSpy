# MythicBee — Content Lanes

Three separate content lanes, not one list.

---

## Lane 2: Social-Native Tropes

Format is the joke. Doesn't require meme knowledge.

| Trope | Voice/register axis | Why it works |
|-------|--------------------| ------------|
| **"be me" greentext** | Cinematic epic narrator | Zero-cost. Format is inherently absurd before you add jokes. |
| **"POV: you're—"** | First-person camera | Instant relatability. No third-person joke setup needed. |
| **"Tell me you're X without telling me"** | Split scene | Massively recognisable. Self-captioning. |
| **"Nobody: / Absolutely nobody:"** | Escalating captions | Cheap. Text-driven. No extra generation needed. |
| **ChatGPT-speak parody** | Flat corporate-AI voice | Very online-recognisable. Dead cheap. Voice/register joke — can bolt onto ANY format. |
| **Documentary narrator** | Attenborough-dry | Narrator voice does ALL the comedic work. Visuals can be dead simple. |
| **Group chat screenshot** | iMessage/WhatsApp UI | Doesn't need video generation. Cheapest possible test. |
| **Corporate training video** | Deadpan HR narrator | Mocks a current genre. |
| **Fake movie trailer** | Epic VO + music sting | Complete, recognisable format on its own. |

**Pros:** Doesn't require meme knowledge. Format IS the joke.
**Cons:** Some are very online-specific.

---

## Lane 3: Tension/Misdirection Skits

**No meme-literacy required. Purely a filmmaking trick.**

Universal comedic timing. Works on anyone.

| Skit | Tension | Reveal |
|------|---------|--------|
| **CoD door** | Fear (gunshots, screams) | Kids playing video games, "turn it down" |
| **Guilty dog** | Suspicion (moans from bedroom) | Dog bingeing Netflix on the bed |
| **Burglar growling** | Fear (aggressive noises) | Dog wrestling with crisp packet |
| **Police lights** | Dread (flashing at 2am) | Dog sat on TV remote, rave scene |
| **Screaming from bathroom** | Horror | Someone stubbed their toe |
| **Sirens outside** | Tension | Ice cream van |

**Pros:** Universal. Works on anyone regardless of online literacy. Least trend-dependent. Will still work in a year.
**Cons:** Requires filmmaking instinct (timing, build-up, reveal).

## The Two Independent Axes

Not just a list of templates. Two independent variables:

### Axis 1: Scene/Format
The situation: greentext, POV, nature doc, trailer, group chat, misdirection skit

### Axis 2: Voice/Register
The narrator: cinematic epic, ChatGPT-flat, Attenborough-dry, hypebeast-hype

**Cross them for more combinations at same generation cost:**

| Scene × Voice | Result |
|---------------|--------|
| Nature doc + ChatGPT voice | "Here we observe Tom. He is 30. This is suboptimal." |
| Trailer + Attenborough voice | "In a world where nobody told him he was getting old…" |
| Greentext + hypebeast voice | "be me. be absolutely iconic. turn 30. mfw." |
| Misdirection + corporate AI voice | "Certainly! Here's a breakdown of why your dog is watching TV." |

**The strategic question:** Is the joke the scene or the voice?

If the joke is the scene → customers pick "formats"
If the joke is the voice → customers pick "voices"

Testing this tells you how to build the product.
