# MythicBee — Pricing Architecture

## Product Stack

| Tier | Price | What it is |
|------|-------|------------|
| **Quick Clip** | £4.99 | 5-10 sec, one template, MP4 |
| **Birthday Pack** | £9.99 | Clip + custom song + e-card + vertical + landscape |
| **Delivered Card** | £14.99 | Everything above + physical QR card mailed |
| **Mini Movie** | £24.99 | 30-60 sec, multiple scenes, more personalization |

---

## Post-Purchase Upsells (Nearly Pure Margin)

Hard personalization work already done. Impulse add-ons:

| Add-on | Price | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Dog singing Happy Birthday | £2.99 | Emotional, shareable |
| Physical card | £4.99 | Tangible, QR to movie |
| Custom song | £2.99 | Zero marginal cost |
| Phone wallpaper | £1.49 | Shareable, brand exposure |
| Another version for group chat | £1.99 | Viral, 10-30 new viewers |

**These are nearly pure margin because the creative work is done.**

---

## Early-Bird Schedule

> **It pays to be organised.**

| Timing | Discount |
|--------|----------|
| 21+ days before | 40% off |
| 14-20 days before | 25% off |
| 7-13 days before | Normal price |
| 48 hours before | +£4 rush fee |

---

## Digital vs Physical Delivery

| Option | Speed | Use case |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Digital now | Immediate | Forgot the birthday |
| Physical card tomorrow | Next day | Planned gift |
| Physical card selected date | Custom date | Future occasions |

**MythicBee becomes useful even for last-minute buyers.**

---

## The Product Test

For every new template:

> **Can a buyer see the 3-second demo and immediately think of one specific person to send it to?**

If yes → launch it.
If no → don't.

---

## Economics

| Product | COGS | Margin |
|---------|------|--------|
| £4.99 Quick Clip | ~£0.55 | ~89% |
| £9.99 Birthday Pack | ~£0.90 | ~91% |
| £14.99 Delivered Card | ~£1.95 | ~87% |
| £24.99 Mini Movie | ~£2.80 | ~89% |
| £2.99 Upsell (song) | ~£0.10 | ~97% |
| £4.99 Upsell (card) | ~£1.10 | ~78% |

**Upsells at 78-97% margin are the profit engine.**
