# MythicBee — Launch Decision
**Date:** 2026-09-06 02:45 UTC
**Status:** DECIDED

---

## The two opinions

**Opinion 1:** Launch Dogcasso + GameWinners simultaneously, digital + physical, manual fulfilment.

**Opinion 2:** Launch GameWinners only, digital only, prove demand, expand later.

## What both agree on

- Don't build the full platform yet
- Manual fulfilment is fine
- Test with real orders
- GameWinners is the sharper brand concept
- Dogcasso is the safer cashflow play

## The middle path

**Launch GameWinners as the primary listing.**
**Have Dogcasso ready as the immediate backup.**
**Digital first. Physical added after 5-10 orders.**

---

## The brand structure

```
MYTHICBEE (brand)
├── GameWinners (hero product — sports legends)
├── Dogcasso (cashflow product — pet portraits)
├── CoverStar (magazine covers)
├── Hall of Fame (awards)
└── ... more later
```

Dogcasso IS MythicBee. It's not a separate business.
MythicBee turns people, pets and memories into legendary gifts.
GameWinners and Dogcasso are just different creative engines.

## The 7-day plan

### Day 1-2: GameWinners listing
- Create 10 sample images (Dad 60, kid 10, boyfriend 30, dog mascot)
- Write Etsy listing: "Turn Dad Into a Football Legend"
- Price: £5.99 digital / £7.99 deluxe / £8.99 printable
- Manual fulfilment via admin dashboard

### Day 3-4: Dogcasso listing (if GameWinners gets views but no sales)
- Create 10 sample pet portraits
- Write Etsy listing: "Custom Renaissance Pet Portrait"
- Price: £5.99 digital / £7.99 deluxe / £8.99 printable

### Day 5-7: First orders
- Fulfil manually
- Record everything
- Iterate on listing based on data

---

## The question we're answering

> **Will someone pay for a personalized AI gift from us?**

GameWinners answers: "Can we sell an imaginative transformation?"
Dogcasso answers: "Can we sell a prettier version of their photo?"

Both answers are valuable. GameWinners is more ownable.

---

## KPIs for continuing vs pivoting

### Continue with GameWinners if:
- 50+ views in first 3 days
- 5+ favorites
- 1+ sale
- Any message/conversation

### Pivot to Dogcasso if:
- <10 views after 3 days
- 0 favorites
- 0 sales
- "Cool but too expensive" feedback

### Kill the concept if:
- Both get <20 views after 7 days
- 0 sales across both
- No engagement at all
