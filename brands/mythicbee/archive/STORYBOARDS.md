## 1. CHARACTER_TALKS_V1

### Example Product

**The Witch Knows Your Name — £4.99**

### Inputs

- recipient name
- optional age
- 1–3 personal details
- tone: friendly / creepy / funny
- landscape or vertical

No recipient image required.

### 8-Second Storyboard

**0–2 sec**
Dark candlelit cottage.
Witch initially has her back to camera.
Camera slowly pushes toward her.

**2–4 sec**
She stops what she's doing.
Slowly turns.
Looks directly into lens.

**4–8 sec**
> "Jacob… I've been expecting you. Milo tells me you've been causing trouble again."
Tiny smile.
Candle extinguishes.
Black.

- SANTA_TALKS
- WITCH_TALKS
- WIZARD_TALKS
- VAMPIRE_TALKS
- PIRATE_TALKS
- ALIEN_TALKS
- FAIRY_TALKS

## 8. MINI_MOVIE_V1

### Product

**"We Make A Tiny Movie About Them"**

£19.99–£29.99.

**Do not generate 30 seconds as one model call.**
Make it: **3 × 10-second controlled sequences** then assemble.

### Customer Answers

- Who is it?
- What occasion?
- How would you describe them?
- Three funny facts.
- Three things they're proud of.
- Who matters to them?
- Pet?
- Desired ending?

### Example: MEET TOM

**Scene 1 — Origins (10 sec)**
> "Meet Tom."
Childhood/younger imagery transitions into cinematic recreation.
> "For thirty years, Tom has been preparing for this moment."

**Scene 2 — The Evidence (10 sec)**
Three funny details illustrated visually.
> "He has mastered coding, travel…and somehow still hasn't mastered answering WhatsApp."

**Scene 3 — Finale (10 sec)**
Heroic version.
Friends/pet around him.
> "But his greatest adventure is only beginning."
**HAPPY 30TH TOM**
