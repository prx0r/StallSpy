## Rule 1: References Have Jobs

Never: "Use these photos."

Instead:

> Image 1 defines Tom's facial identity and hairstyle.
> Image 2 defines Tom's clothing.
> Image 3 defines the dog.
> Preserve their identities throughout.

Every reference needs:
- **Identity** — who is this person/character
- **Environment** — where does the scene take place
- **Motion** — what action occurs
- **Voice** — who speaks, what they say
- **Style** — visual aesthetic reference

---

## Rule 2: Describe Observable Motion

Bad:
> Santa looks magical.

Good:
> Santa enters from frame left, walks three steps toward the tree, crouches, places one wrapped parcel beneath it, then turns his head toward the camera.

Describe what the camera sees. Not vibes. Actions.

---

## Rule 3: Camera Direction Is Literal

Use actual camera terminology:

- **push in** / **pull out**
- **pan left/right**
- **tracking shot**
- **arc shot**
- **static**
- **dolly**
- **crane**

Specify speed/range when important.

---

## Rule 4: Use Timed Shots

For multi-shot clips:

```text
[Shot 1] ...
[Shot 2] At 00:03.500, the camera cuts to...
[Shot 3] At 00:07.000...
```

MiniMax explicitly documents this structure.

---

## Rule 5: Treat Sound Like Production

Specify separately:

- **dialogue** — who speaks, what they say
- **room ambience** — background atmosphere
- **foley** — specific sound effects
- **music** — score, mood, tempo

H3 generates audio natively. Don't waste that.

---

## Rule 6: Preserve Identities Explicitly

For uploaded people:

> Facial proportions, eye colour, hairstyle, skin tone and distinctive features remain unchanged.

Reference mode is designed around retention/preservation relationships.

---

## Prompt Structure (Recommended)

```text
[Visual Style]
[Scene setting / environment]
[Character descriptions with reference jobs]
[Shot-by-shot action with timestamps]
[Camera movements]
[Dialogue with speaker IDs]
[Sound design layers]
[Music/mood]
[Text overlays — note: apply in post, not in model]
```

---

## What to Apply in Post (FFmpeg)

- Final typography / text overlays
- Color grading
- Subtitles
- Brand watermark
- Aspect ratio composition
- Music bed mixing (if not generated by H3)
- Final encode

**Do not ask H3 to render text if FFmpeg can do it perfectly.**

---

## Experiment Logging

Save for every generation:

- input prompt
- expanded prompt (fal returns this)
- seed
- output video
- human score (1-5)
- generation time
- GPU cost
- reroll count

**That's how the template library becomes a measured production system that learns which recipes reliably make good films.**
