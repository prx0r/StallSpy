# MythicBee — Strategic Thesis

## The Moonpig Validation

Moonpig has already proved almost every boring part of this business:

- **89.4% revenue from existing customers**
- **113 million occasion reminders**
- **~40% of orders within 7 days of a reminder**
- **12.3m active customers, 36m orders, £335.5m revenue (FY26)**
- **19 cards per year per UK card-giving adult**
- **Gifts attached to 17.9% of orders**

**We don't need to prove people want an online gifting assistant.**

We need to prove **personalized mini-movies/memes are a sufficiently compelling acquisition wedge to get younger customers to start their gifting relationship with MythicBee rather than Moonpig.**

---

## The Wedge

Not "better greeting cards."

> **"Make Dave the main character of something hilarious in 60 seconds."**

Discovery:
- Breaking News: Dave Turns 30
- Scientists Have Been Studying Sarah For 28 Years
- Santa Was Caught In Your Living Room
- Your Dog Has Something To Tell You

Upload photo. £4.99. Studio-quality result. Dump in group chat. 10 people see MythicBee.

**That's a vastly more viral acquisition unit than a card.**

---

## The Retention Engine

After checkout:
> "Want us to send this as a real card too? +£4.99"

Now we become Moonpig.

Then:
> "Save Alice so next year is effortless?"

The recipient graph + occasion reminders + proactive suggestions = accounts that get more valuable over time.

**Moonpig makes you start from scratch every year. MythicBee says "Alice turns 30 in 21 days, we've already got 6 ideas ready."**

---

## The CAC Solution

Moonpig owns SEO for:
- `birthday cards`
- `personalised cards`
- `mothers day cards`

We advertise on things they don't own:
- `funny personalized birthday video`
- `custom birthday movie`
- `dog birthday message`
- `santa video for child`
- `funny gift for friend`
- `personalized meme`
- `birthday roast video`

TikTok/Reels becomes natural:
> "POV: your mate buys you the worst birthday gift imaginable."

Then show: BBC-style Breaking News → "Local man Ryan Thompson has somehow survived 31 consecutive years." £4.99.

**People buy because the demo is funny. Then MythicBee converts them into card and gift customers later.**

---

## Target Customer

**The person in the friendship/family group who actually remembers birthdays and buys stuff.**

- Under-55s and parents (Moonpig's highest-propensity segment)
- Already buying online
- Already sending memes
- Already in WhatsApp/Instagram/TikTok group chats
- Wants something funny/personal but doesn't want to spend 45 minutes prompting Gemini

---

## The Flywheel

```text
Buyer purchases for Tom
        ↓
Tom receives gift, watches movie
        ↓
Tom sees "Make one for someone you love"
        ↓
Tom buys for Sarah
        ↓
Sarah receives gift, watches movie
        ↓
...
```

Each customer → recipient → new customer.

**10-30 people see the brand from one purchase.**

Greeting-card purchase: 1 recipient.
Funny personalized video: family WhatsApp + Instagram + TikTok + work Slack + group chat.

**Viral coefficient materially better than physical cards.**

## Defensibility Layers

1. **Recipient graph** — Who they buy for, what they like, what worked
2. **Template library** — 500+ proven scenarios, each improving with models
3. **Rendering engine** — Gets better every year without customer effort
4. **Personalization history** — "Last year you sent the Documentary"
5. **Recommendation engine** — "Frank has prepared a speech" (based on profile)
6. **Viral loop** — Recipients become buyers via gift pages

**After a year: "Tom turns 30 in 21 days. Based on last year's Documentary format, here are 3 new ideas we think he'll love."**

**That's not something Moonpig or any competitor can replicate quickly.**

---

## The Bigger Concept

MythicBee isn't ultimately:
> AI cards.

It's:
> **A memory-aware gifting account.**

You tell us about the people you love **once**.

After that, every birthday, Christmas, anniversary, graduation and random joke becomes almost one-click.

And because the production engine improves every year, Tom's 2030 birthday movie should be dramatically better than his 2026 one without the user learning anything new.

**That recurring recipient graph + template library + rendering engine is the most defensible version of the whole idea.**
