# EasyEtsy Meme Product Strategy & 67 Plan

## Cards make the meme thesis stronger

Every animation naturally has an iconic freeze-frame.

The product becomes:

> **You become the meme → 5–10 second animation → iconic freeze-frame → same freeze-frame becomes the physical card → QR opens the full clip.**

Design every Dogcasso meme recipe to output **both video and print artwork from day one**. We don't necessarily need to list every card immediately, but the creative work is already done.

---

## Meme selection: what's viral ≠ what makes a good product

In 2026, many Meme-of-the-Month winners have been fandom-, song- or event-dependent: August's was *Lesbian Space Princess* fandom war, July's was Jimothy the raccoon, June's was the song-driven *Du Bist Gut Genug*, March's was a *Chainsaw Man* panel. Useful for temporary organic posts, terrible foundations for permanent Etsy SKUs. ([Know Your Meme][1])

**67 is the huge exception.** KYM's #1 meme of 2025, main entry still being updated in August 2026, spawned further 2026 participatory trends such as "United by 67." **67 gets a proper product now.** ([Know Your Meme][2])

## The animation trick

These shouldn't look like "AI recreated an internet meme."

They should initially look like **a normal movie clip**. Then gradually the viewer realizes what they're watching.

### Two Buttons

Dave walks into control room.

Technician: "You have two options."

Camera reveals:

**ACT YOUR AGE** and **HAVE ANOTHER PINT**

Dave starts sweating. Slow push-in. Hand drifts toward pint.

**FREEZE FRAME.**

Then: **HAPPY 30TH DAVE**

That transition from **realistic moving world → iconic meme composition** is the Dogcasso magic.

## Template deep-dives

### THIS_IS_FINE

Don't use the original cartoon dog (KC Green's *Gunshow* comic). ([Know Your Meme][5])

Sarah sits in immaculate kitchen drinking coffee.

Birthday balloon behind her pops. Cake catches fire. Someone runs past screaming. Ceiling starts leaking. Dog steals cake.

Sarah slowly looks into camera. Takes another sip. Freeze.

> **SARAH PREPARING TO HOST HER 30TH**

Card: front = freeze-frame. Inside: "It is fine. Happy 30th." QR opens full clip.

### I'M_IN_DANGER (Ralph bus)

Not Ralph himself. Photographic/live-action.

Tom sitting on bus, headphones in, content.

Phone notification: **"30 TOMORROW 🎂"**

Tom stops smiling. Slowly looks out window. Tiny nervous chuckle.

Freeze. **TOM, AGE 29 YEARS 364 DAYS**

Probably a 5-second render. KYM still regards the Ralph bus shot as a confirmed, highly recognizable reaction format in 2026. ([Know Your Meme][6])

### TWO_BUTTONS

Nearly ideal. The buyer writes the joke themselves.

**30th:** Start behaving like an adult vs. Go out Friday

**Dad:** Save money vs. Buy another power tool

**Mum:** Relax vs. Rearrange the entire house

**Teenager:** Do homework vs. 67

Animate their actual face sweating over it. ([Know Your Meme][3])

### CAUGHT_LOOKING

Highest viral ceiling despite being harder.

Boyfriend + girlfriend walking. Looks like ordinary phone video.

Woman walks past carrying: **"ANOTHER £700 LEGO SET"**

Boyfriend's neck slowly turns. Girlfriend realizes. Freeze. Labels appear.

Birthday version: Boyfriend = Tom, Walking partner = Being responsible at 30, Passing temptation = Going Ibiza with the lads.

*Motion preceding the original still* is what makes it funny. ([Know Your Meme][4])

### WOMAN_YELLING_AT_CAT

Buyer uploads: **her + her actual cat/dog.**

Woman furiously explaining. Cat at dining table. Completely unmoved.

Woman: "You literally ate five minutes ago!"

Cat: "Allegedly."

Freeze into familiar left/right composition.

More personal because **that's literally their own pet arguing with them.** ([Know Your Meme][7])

### MASTER_PLAN

No Gru. Recipient in dramatic underground planning room.

Board: STEP 1 — TURN 30 → flip → STEP 2 — BECOME MATURE → flip → STEP 3 — ?????

Recipient keeps speaking. Stops. Slowly looks back. Looks at camera.

Gru's Plan works because the presenter encounters the unexpected third panel and stares back in confusion. ([Know Your Meme][8])

### DISASTER_PERSON

Almost made for generative video.

Birthday person turns toward camera. Behind them: birthday tent blows over, cake falls, champagne explodes, dad runs past.

Recipient gives tiny smug smile. Freeze.

> **"SARAH'S 30TH WENT REALLY WELL."**

One photo input. Excellent economics. ([Know Your Meme][9])

### CONFUSED_MATHS

Ultra-cheap. One face input.

Recipient hears: "If you're 30 today and you've been saying you're 25 for six years…"

Expression changes. Camera slowly pushes. Equations appear around them **in deterministic postproduction**.

Original meme added maths overlays later—the source TV scene was simply a confused woman. ([Know Your Meme][10])

### EPIC_HANDSHAKE

Two uploaded people meet. Dramatic slow motion. Ridiculous handshake.

Labels: Sarah / Emma / centre: HATING JAMES' NEW HAIRCUT

Classic meme is literally about two sides finding a point of agreement. ([Know Your Meme][11])

### TRADE_OFFER

Almost free to produce. Originally spread as TikTok format. ([Know Your Meme][12])

Recipient walks up seriously. "I have a proposal." Freeze.

**I RECEIVE:** Another year of life. **YOU RECEIVE:** Back pain.

### 67

Own weird little product family. Don't use Skrilla's copyrighted original recording.

Use the **number + participatory gesture + original Dogcasso audio/sound design**.

For a 7-year-old:

Parent: "How old is he now?" Other parent: "Six?"

Kid slowly enters frame. "SEVEN?" Kid: **"SIX SEVEN!"** gesture.

Suddenly six/seven copies of kid appear doing it. Confetti.

> **HAPPY 7TH JACOB**

The bizarre ambiguity of "6-7" and the palm-up alternating gesture are core to the meme's spread among Gen Alpha/Gen Z. ([Know Your Meme][2])

## Current 2026 research: do NOT chase every current meme

Italian Brainrot is culturally recognizable, but much of it is tied to specific community-created AI characters and source audio; 2026's monthly winners often depend on copyrighted songs, films, manga or specific viral personalities. ([Know Your Meme][13])

Dogcasso should have:

**80% evergreen meme grammars**

Two Buttons, This Is Fine, Caught Looking, I'm In Danger, Disaster, Confused Maths, Trade Offer, Master Plan, Handshake, etc.

**20% rapid Trend Drops**

67 today. Whatever suddenly becomes massive next month.

Produce the Trend Drop as an **organic Dogcasso advert first**. Only if it gets tags/shares/comments like "I NEED THIS FOR JAKE" do we turn it into a proper Etsy SKU.

## Cards absolutely belong

Don't build "cards" separately.

Every template manifest should produce:

```text
DOGCASSO_TWO_BUTTONS_V1

→ 9:16 video
→ 16:9 video
→ 1:1 social still
→ 5x7 card front
→ card-inside artwork
→ thumbnail
→ QR destination
```

The **iconic freeze-frame is automatically the card design**.

Etsy listing:

### £4.99

**Make Yourself The Two Buttons Meme — Video**

### £9.99

**Personalised Two Buttons Birthday Card + Animated Movie**

Same creative engine. Card customer scans it and sees the scene come alive.

## If spending fal money tomorrow

Test **six**, in this order:

**67 → Two Buttons → This Is Fine → Dog vs Owner → I'm In Danger → Caught Looking.**

Not because Breaking News is bad—it is validated—but because these are much more **Dogcasso**.

Breaking News feels like an existing personalized-video product.

> **"Become the meme"**

feels like something people haven't had before.

The most important creative rule:

> **Every Dogcasso meme should begin as a believable normal scene and end on a frame the internet instantly recognizes.**

That is the exact gimmick for the first advertising campaign.

Monitor current meme sources and flag new formats that fit this **personalizable action-snapshot** structure.
