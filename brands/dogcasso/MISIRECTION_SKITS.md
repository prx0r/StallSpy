# Dogcasso — Misdirection Skits

**Lane 3: Tension/Misdirection**

No meme-literacy required. Purely a filmmaking trick. Universal comedic timing. Works on anyone.

---

## Skit 1: CoD Door

### Concept

Tense build-up → mundane reveal. Kids playing video games, not actual danger.

### 5-Second Version

**0–2 sec — Tense Build**
Distant bangs. A scream. Dead silence in hallway.
Camera: handheld, shaky, approaching door.
Sound: muffled game audio, heartbeat.

**2–4 sec — Door Opens**
Hand on door handle. Held breath. Door swings open.
Camera: POV push through doorway.

**4–5 sec — Reveal**
Three kids sat calmly on sofa. Controllers in hand.
One turns around annoyed:
> "Can you turn it down? We're trying to hear footsteps."
Camera: static wide shot.
Sound: game audio becomes clear.

### Variants

- **Screaming from bathroom** → someone stubbed their toe
- **Sirens outside, everyone rushes to window** → ice cream van
- **Horror-movie creak on stairs** → it's the dog

## Skit 2: Guilty Dog

### Concept

Suspicion build-up → dog watching TV. Zero risk/edge. The "you got me" face is hugely shareable.

### 7-Second Version

**0–2 sec — Key in Door**
Sound: key turning, door opening.
Footsteps creaking up stairs faster than normal.
Breathing audible.
Tinny "moaning-adjacent" sound leaking from behind bedroom door.
Camera: handheld, following footsteps up stairs.

**2–4 sec — Hand on Handle**
Close-up of hand reaching for bedroom door handle.
The "moans" are louder — clearly a love scene on TV.
Camera: push in on handle. Pause.

**4–6 sec — Door Opens**
Dog, sat upright on the bed, remote in paw (or between paws).
Mid-episode of some overwrought Netflix drama.
The "moans" are just a love scene on TV.

**6–7 sec — The Face**
Dog turns. Ears drop. Slow blink.
Classic "well, you got me" caught-red-handed face.
But he's just been caught bingeing.

### Caption

**"WHEN THE DOG GETS THE WIFI PASSWORD"**

or personalised:

**"[NAME] COMING HOME EARLY, EXPECTATIONS VS THE DOG"**

### Why This Works

- Zero risk — nothing actually happens, it's a dog watching telly
- The "you got me" face is a hugely shareable, recognisable expression
- People love captioning guilty-dog content — proven organic genre
- Dog IS the punchline — no need to insert recipient's face for demo
- Cheaper to test than multi-person skits

## Production Notes

### Why Lane 3 is the priority

- **Universal:** Works on anyone regardless of online literacy
- **Trend-independent:** Will still work in a year
- **Cheap:** Mostly relies on timing and reveal, not complex generation
- **Shareable:** Tension/release is inherently shareable
- **Dogcasso-friendly:** Dog can be the punchline in most variants

### Generation approach

1. **Build tension** (2-3 sec): handheld camera, muffled sounds, slow approach
2. **Door/opening moment** (1 sec): the threshold between tension and reveal
3. **Reveal** (2-3 sec): static wide shot, mundane reality, caption

### Voice options for these skits

| Voice | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| None (just SFX) | Pure visual comedy |
| Cinematic narrator | "In a world where..." irony |
| News reporter | "Breaking: local dog caught watching Netflix" |
| ChatGPT voice | "Certainly! Here's why your dog is on the bed." |

### Cross with voice axis

Same skit + different voice = different joke:

- CoD door + Attenborough voice: "Here we observe the juvenile human in its natural habitat..."
- Guilty dog + ChatGPT voice: "I understand you have concerns about the dog. Let me explain."
- Burglar + hypebeast voice: "YO this dog is absolutely WILD with the crisp packet rn"
