# Dogcasso — 8 Canonical Engines

Everything seasonal and trendy becomes **content**, not engineering.

---

## Engine Matrix

| Engine | User supplies | Output | Core products |
|--------|--------------|--------|---------------|
| **CHARACTER_TALKS** | name + facts | character addresses recipient | Santa, witch, wizard, vampire |
| **IN_YOUR_ROOM** | room photo | impossible visitor appears | Santa, ghost, monster, dinosaur |
| **PUT_ME_IN** | face/body photo | recipient stars in scenario | memes, sports, fantasy |
| **BREAKING_NEWS** | face + funny fact | fake news segment | birthdays, promotions, roasts |
| **DOCUMENTARY** | 3–5 photos + facts | serious narration about ridiculous person | birthdays, retirement |
| **ROAST_PANEL** | 2–5 people/pets | characters roast recipient | family, friends, dog |
| **TRAILER** | face + facts | cinematic movie trailer | birthday, wedding, graduation |
| **MINI_MOVIE** | 5–10 photos + story | 30–60 sec multi-scene film | premium birthday/wedding/etc. |
