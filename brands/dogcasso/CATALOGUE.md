# Dogcasso — Product Catalogue

## Three Catalogue Layers

All three run through the same handful of pipelines. A trend isn't a new engineering project — it's **new script + new storyboard + existing face insertion workflow.**

---

## Layer 1: Classics (Evergreen)

Meme grammars people have known for 5-15 years. Permanent inventory that improves with models.

| Meme Grammar | Our Personalized Movie | Best Occasions | Upload |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Had To Do It To Em** | Recipient walks dramatically into frame, does the pose. "You know James had to turn 30 on 'em." | Birthday, graduation, wedding entrance, promotion | Full body |
| **Disaster Girl** | Recipient calmly turns to camera while party/wedding/office explodes into absurd chaos behind them | Birthday roast, retirement, stag/hen, new home | Face |
| **Distracted Boyfriend** | Three uploaded people recreate the turn-around scene with custom labels/story | Couples, wedding, birthday roast | 3 faces |
| **Success Kid** | Recipient does ridiculous victory celebration after accomplishing something mundane | Graduation, exam, new job, sobriety milestone, birthday | Face/body |
| **Two Buttons** | Recipient sweats choosing between two personalized options | Birthday, wedding, Christmas, office joke | Face |
| **This Is Fine** | Recipient calmly drinks tea while their party/kitchen/workplace collapses around them | 30th/40th birthday, parenthood, retirement | Face/body |
| **Change My Mind** | Recipient at table with personalized sign; increasingly ridiculous people challenge them | Birthday roast, wedding, friend-group joke | Face |
| **Roll Safe** | Recipient taps temple while narrator delivers intentionally terrible logic | Birthdays, students, work colleagues | Face |
| **Woman Yelling / Cat** | Friend/partner argues dramatically with recipient's actual cat/dog across dinner table | Pet owner, couples, birthdays | 1 person + pet |
| **Master Plan Fail** | Recipient explains their perfect four-step birthday/wedding plan; step 3 catastrophically invalidates everything | Birthday, wedding, stag, work | Face/full body |
| **Expanding Brain** | Multiple increasingly cosmic versions of recipient reveal progressively sillier choices | Birthday, gift reveal, graduation | Face |
| **Gigachad Transformation** | Recipient starts ordinary, progressively transforms into absurdly cinematic legendary version | Birthday, gym friend, graduation, best man | Face/body |

### Legal Note

Recreate the **meme grammar** with the customer's own people/rooms/pets. Don't animate the original copyrighted photo, actor, or movie still. The recipient becomes the meme. This makes the product better.

## Layer 2: Trending (Weekly Refresh)

Formats from TikTok/Instagram/KnowYourMeme converted into personalized scenarios while they're hot.

**Pipeline:**
```text
TikTok Creative Center
        +
KnowYourMeme new entries
        +
Reddit
        +
Google Trends
        +
Etsy Marketplace Insights
        ↓
TREND RADAR
        ↓
Could this become a personalized video?
        ↓
YES
        ↓
clone nearest workflow
        ↓
make demo
        ↓
Etsy listing + TikTok demo
```

**Format insight to steal (not the audio):**
- "replacing questions" → setup → recipient-specific info → unexpected punchline
- "Will He Perform" → show someone failing at the crucial moment
- Absurd personalization → simple reusable comedic structures
