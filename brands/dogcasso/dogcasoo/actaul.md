# Dogcasso: The Artifact Abstraction

## The better abstraction

> **What fictional object/world would someone desperately want themselves, their partner, their kid or their pet to exist inside?**

Dogcasso makes that artifact real, and AI lets you add layers that previously would have required an illustrator + animator + musician + editor.

The portrait is almost the **identity-generation primitive** underneath everything.

---

## three.ws is exactly the thing

It can currently turn photos/prompts into **rigged GLB avatars**, generate talking-avatar videos, embed avatars on websites, and put those avatars into AR through WebXR/Quick Look without an app. It even supports walking an avatar around a real room on mobile. ([three.ws][1])

So eventually:

**photo → Dogcasso portrait → 3D version → physical card → scan → character appears in room**

isn't science fiction. It's already technically buildable.

But don't disappear into 3D infrastructure yet.

## Tarot is ridiculously well suited

Because the **card itself already has meaning**.

Imagine sending three facts:

> Jeff
> Labrador
> steals unattended food

Dogcasso returns:

### **THE DEVOURER**

*A card of abundance, appetite and catastrophic failure to secure the kitchen.*

**Upright:** enthusiasm, opportunity, second breakfast
**Reversed:** empty bowl, closed refrigerator, betrayal

Then you get:

- beautiful tarot artwork
- printable high-res card
- 15-sec animation
- atmospheric soundtrack
- optional physical foil card
- eventually AR version

There are already fresh Etsy listings for bespoke tarot cards based on personal themes, pets and symbols, so customers already understand personalized tarot as a product. ([Etsy][2])

Dogcasso adds the spectacle.

## Music: don't sell "AI songs" — use music to make another purchase feel absurdly good value

There are already lots of Etsy sellers offering personalized birthday/pet/anniversary songs around roughly **$12–25+**, typically asking for the recipient, memories, jokes, preferred genre etc. ([Etsy][3])

The niche still looks comparatively immature. No personalized-song equivalent of the 44,700-sale pet-portrait monster found.

That makes **bundling** more interesting.

### £6.99 — YOUR DOG AS AN ALBUM

Upload Jeff.

You receive:

**Album cover:**
*JEFF — Unsupervised in the Kitchen*

**Track:**
*Who's A Good Boy? (Jeff's Version)*

An actual 45–90 second personalized song about:

- Jeff stealing socks
- Jeff loving Mum
- Jeff hating the postman
- Jeff's birthday
- whatever they've told us

Plus:

**15-second music video.**

That's a much more remarkable £6.99 purchase than a JPEG.

Etsy custom-song sellers already bundle **cover artwork + lyrics + MP3**, useful validation of the packaging idea. ([Etsy][3])

## Formalize the idea generator

Look for intersections of:

**PROVEN OBSESSION** × **RECOGNIZABLE ARTIFACT** × **RECIPIENT** × **OCCASION** × **NEW AI CAPABILITY**

Example:

Football × rookie card × Dad × Father's Day × video =

**Dad's Legendary 1998 Season**

Upload Dad's photo → beautiful retro sports card → scan/watch him score the winning goal → fake commentator loses his mind → personalized stadium chant.

Extraordinary Father's Day gift compared with another mug.

Or:

Fantasy × tarot × girlfriend × birthday × image/music/video =

**Her personalized Major Arcana card**, with an original mystical theme and moving portrait.

That framework will produce hundreds of ideas.

## One-product simplicity is still the lesson

The clearest successful-shop example remains PawtraitDesignCo:

**44.7k sales. 9.5k reviews. 4.9 stars. One listing.**

$9.99 custom pet portrait. ([Etsy][4])

Reviews contain exactly what you'd hope:

people buying again for another dog, using it repeatedly for gifts, praising speed, likeness and value. ([Etsy][4])

Dogcasso shouldn't initially look like an AI casino with 70 styles.

Extremely strong core:

> **We turn your photo into this.**

Relentlessly show: **REAL PHOTO → ACTUAL RESULT**

Then make the bonus stack unexpectedly large:

### £5.99-ish core

**Dogcasso Portrait**

- high-res portrait
- social version
- 15-sec living portrait

### £8.99-ish

**Dogcasso Experience**

- portrait
- animation
- personalized mini-theme/song
- digital card

### £15–30+

**Physical Gift**

- everything above
- printed card/foil card/poster/etc.

The music/video costs are tiny relative to the **perceived amount of creative work** the recipient sees.

That's the arbitrage.

## One caveat on the songs

Use a generation provider/plan whose outputs explicitly permit commercial use.

Suno's current paid tiers grant commercial-use rights to eligible downloaded songs, including independent sale and use in videos/games, while its free outputs are non-commercial. Suno also explicitly warns that commercial-use rights don't necessarily mean the AI output itself receives copyright protection. ([Suno Help][5])

Build it cleanly rather than casually generating on free accounts and reselling.
