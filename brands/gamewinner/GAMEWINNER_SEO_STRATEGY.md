## 2. Title Formulas

Etsy's algorithm gives **most weight to the first 40 characters** of the title. Front-load the primary keyword.

### Formula: `Product Type + Personalization + Occasion + Recipient`

**Football (soccer):**
```
Personalized Football Video Gift | Dad Scores The Winning Goal | Custom Birthday Movie for Him
```

**Football (American):**
```
Custom Football Birthday Video | Put Him In The Game | Personalized Gift for Dad Husband
```

**Baseball:**
```
Personalized Baseball Video Gift | Custom Birthday Movie | He Hits The Walk-Off Winner
```

**Basketball:**
```
Custom Basketball Video Gift | Championship Buzzer Beater | Personalized Birthday Movie
```

**Cricket:**
```
Personalised Cricket Video Gift | He Hits The Winning Six | Custom Birthday Film for Dad
```

**Father's Day variants:**
```
Father's Day Football Gift | Personalized Video | Dad Scores The Winner | Custom Movie
```

**Birthday variants:**
```
50th Birthday Football Gift | Personalized Video | Dad Scores The Winning Goal | Custom Movie
```

**Retirement variants:**
```
Retirement Sports Video Gift | Custom Movie | Put Him In The Moment | Personalised Gift
```

### Title rules
- Lead with `Personalized` or `Custom` — buyers filter for this
- Include sport + product type in first 40 chars
- Add occasion + recipient after the pipe separator
- Use `|` separators for readability (Etsy treats them as spaces)
- UK spelling `Personalised` for UK/international listings

## 6. Listing Quality Score Optimization

Etsy's 2026 algorithm heavily weights engagement signals. Here's how to maximize:

### Photos (CRITICAL)
- **First image:** High-contrast hero shot — recipient's face in the winning moment. Must be scroll-stopping at thumbnail size.
- **Image 2-3:** Show the video playing on a phone/TV (proof it's real)
- **Image 4-5:** Multiple sport options (show range)
- **Image 6:** Physical gift card/mug mockup (if offering Gift Set)
- **Image 7:** Customer reaction screenshot (social proof)
- **Image 8:** "How it works" infographic (reduce friction)
- **Image 9-10:** Different recipient examples (dad, husband, grandad)

### Video (MANDATORY)
- 15-30 second clip showing a generated video playing
- Show the recipient's face appearing in the sports moment
- Text overlay: "Upload a photo → He scores the winning goal"
- Listings with video get **significantly higher** quality scores

### Description Structure
```text
1. HOOK (first 160 chars — appears in search snippets)
   "Put [Name] in the moment he's dreamed about since he was eight.
    Upload a photo. We make him score the winning goal."

2. HOW IT WORKS (3 steps)
   1. Upload a clear photo
   2. Tell us the sport, name, and occasion
   3. Receive your personalized video in 24-48 hours

3. WHAT YOU GET
   - [X]-second personalized sports video
   - HD download (16:9 and 9:16 formats)
   - Hero portrait from the winning moment
   - Gift page at mythicbee.com

4. PERSONALIZATION OPTIONS
   - Sport: Football, Baseball, Basketball, Cricket
   - Occasion: Birthday, Father's Day, Christmas, Retirement
   - Name & shirt number
   - Team colours
   - Inside joke for commentary

5. FAQ
   - How do I send my photo?
   - What kind of photo works best?
   - How long does it take?
   - Can I include multiple people?

6. ABOUT GAME WINNER
   We put the person you love into the sporting moment
   they've dreamed about their whole life.
```

### Personalization Field
Enable Etsy's personalization field with clear instructions:
```
Name: (e.g., "Steve")
Sport: (Football / Baseball / Basketball / Cricket)
Occasion: (Birthday / Father's Day / Christmas / Retirement)
Age/Milestone: (e.g., "60th birthday")
Team Colours: (e.g., "England red & white")
Shirt Name & Number: (e.g., "PRIOR / 60")
Inside joke for commentator: (e.g., "Dad claims his knee stopped him turning professional in 1987")
```

High engagement on the personalization field = Etsy treats it as a "true" custom item = higher ranking.

## 10. Review Velocity Strategy

Reviews are the **#1 conversion factor** for custom products. Accelerate them:

1. **Deliver fast** — 24-48 hours, not 1 week
2. **Include a "Thank You" note** asking for a photo review
3. **Follow up** 3 days after delivery: "How did [Name] react?"
4. **Respond to every review** — signals active shop
5. **Photo reviews** are gold — incentivize with a free wallpaper upgrade
6. **Target 10 reviews in first 30 days** — this is the inflection point
