# Prodigi catalogue for MogMug — count, categories, picks

Source: prodigi.com/products + /downloads asset lists (2026-09-12). Truth for live SKUs/prices: Product Details + Quote endpoints.

## How many can we personalise?

- **200+ base products** (Prodigi's figure), **500,000+ with variants** (sizes/finishes/regions).
- Personalisable = effectively **all of them**: the whole model is your-artwork-on-their-blank.
  ~90 named blanks appear across their photography/template lists; the rest are size/finish/region variants.
- Constraint that matters isn't count, it's **portal fit**: flat printable area for the trigger + giftable +
  worldwide fulfilment. That narrows 200+ to ~30 serious candidates below.

## Categories (portal fit in brackets)

1. **Drinkware** — photo/magic/latte/travel mugs, water bottles. (Best: daily ritual object, big print area.)
2. **Cards & stationery** — greetings cards (direct/self-send), postcards, notebooks, calendars, wrapping paper. (Best: cheapest portal, gifting flow.)
3. **Wall art** — posters, canvas, framed/metal/wood prints, photo tiles. (Best: presence, margin.)
4. **Soft home** — cushions, blankets, towels, duvet/pillow, tapestry, aprons, coasters, placemats. (Best: perceived value.)
5. **Pet-specific** — dog/pet bandanas, metal pet tags. (Best: on-theme, photo flywheel.)
6. **Seasonal** — ceramic/alu/glass ornaments, baubles, sacks, candles. (Best: gifting spikes.)
7. **Wearables** — tees (Gildan 64000), totes, socks, patches, pins. (Best: walking billboards.)
8. **Tech** — phone/iPad cases, watch straps, mats. (Best: daily visibility.)
9. **Play** — jigsaws, playing cards, paddles, skateboards. (Best: Pogtown games tie-in later.)
10. **Keepsake** — photo books, magnets, stickers, tins, clocks. (Best: AOV add-ons.)
